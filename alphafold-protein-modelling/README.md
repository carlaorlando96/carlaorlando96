# AlphaFold-Assisted Protein Modelling

## Overview

Application of AlphaFold-based structure prediction and computational modelling to investigate enzyme structure and function during my PhD research.

## Methods and Tools

- **AlphaFold:** protein structure prediction.
- **Structural analysis:** inspection of predicted structures, confidence assessment and structure preparation.
- **Molecular docking:** investigation of protein–ligand interactions.
- **Molecular dynamics:** simulations of apo protein systems.
- **Comparative modelling:** structural comparison with related bacterial proteins.
- **Metalloprotein modelling:** incorporation of relevant metal ions during protein structure preparation.

## My Contribution

Performed protein structure prediction, inspection and preparation, followed by downstream computational studies. Adapted the structural modelling workflow to the requirements of different enzyme systems, including active-site analysis and investigation of molecular interactions.

## Research Context

PhD research in Computational Chemistry, University of Milano-Bicocca (2022–2026).
