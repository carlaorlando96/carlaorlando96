# Carla Orlando

**Computational Chemist - Molecular Modelling - Biomolecular Simulation**

## About Me

I am a computational chemist with a PhD in Chemical Sciences from the University of Milano-Bicocca, completed in February 2026.
My research focuses on computational studies of biomolecular systems, with particular experience in molecular dynamics simulations, molecular docking, protein–ligand interactions and computational enzymology.
During my PhD, I used computational approaches to investigate protein structure, enzyme mechanisms and molecular interactions, combining molecular modelling, simulation and structural analysis.
