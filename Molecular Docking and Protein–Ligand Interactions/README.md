# Molecular Docking and Protein–Ligand Interactions

## Overview

Molecular docking studies were performed to investigate protein–ligand binding modes and characterize interactions between proteins and their ligands.

## Computational Methods

- **Docking software:** AutoDock, AutoDock Vina and Schrödinger Glide.
- **Binding pose analysis:** Evaluation and inspection of predicted ligand-binding poses.
- **Interaction analysis:** Identification of hydrogen bonds and hydrophobic interactions.
- **Active site characterization:** Analysis of ligand interactions with key residues within protein binding sites.
- **Complex preparation:** Preparation of protein–ligand complexes for subsequent molecular dynamics simulations.

## Research Focus

The computational studies focused on characterizing protein–ligand recognition, binding modes, and molecular interactions within protein active sites.

## Research Context

PhD research in Computational Chemistry, University of Milano-Bicocca (2022–2026).
