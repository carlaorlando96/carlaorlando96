# Quantum Chemical Calculations (QM)

## Overview

Quantum chemical calculations were applied to investigate molecular properties, reaction mechanisms, and reactivity in bioinorganic and biochemical systems.

## Computational Methods

- **Density Functional Theory (DFT):** Investigation of enzymatic reaction mechanisms and reaction pathways.
- **QM cluster models:** Modelling of metalloenzyme active sites, including copper-containing catalytic centres.
- **Electronic structure analysis:** Evaluation of molecular geometries, electronic properties, and reaction energetics.
- **Reaction mechanisms:** Investigation of oxidation pathways, hydrogen transfer, and radical-mediated processes.
- **Quantum chemical calculations for molecular parametrization:** Geometry optimization and electronic structure calculations for substrate parameter development.
