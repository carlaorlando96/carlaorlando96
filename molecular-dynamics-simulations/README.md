# Molecular Dynamics Simulations of Enzymatic Systems

## Overview

Molecular dynamics simulations were used to investigate the structural stability, flexibility, and dynamic behaviour of enzymes and metalloenzymes in complex with different substrates.

## Computational Methods

- **Simulation packages:** GROMACS and AMBER.
- **Structural stability and flexibility:** Root Mean Square Deviation (RMSD) and Root Mean Square Fluctuation (RMSF).
- **Structural analysis:** Secondary structure evolution and hydrogen-bond analysis.
- **Interaction analysis:** Monitoring of interatomic and bond distances.
- **Collective motions:** Principal Component Analysis (PCA).
- **Binding energy estimation:** MM-PBSA and MM-GBSA calculations.

## Research Focus

The simulations and trajectory analyses were used to investigate enzyme dynamics, structural changes, substrate interactions, and the energetic properties of enzyme–substrate complexes.

## Research Context
